# Shapka privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Shapka is a free party game. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases,
no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

## What the game uses while you play

To run a game, the app sends the following to the Shapka game server:

- the **name** you choose for the game (it can be anything, such as a nickname),
- the **words** you add to the hat,
- game actions such as starting a turn or marking a word as guessed.

Other players in the same game can see your name, and the words when the game
reveals them. A game can only be joined with its game code, and the host can
remove any player.

This game data is kept **only in the server's memory** while the game is
running. It is never written to a database or disk, is not linked to your
identity or device, and is lost when the server restarts or goes to sleep,
which happens shortly after nobody is playing.

To protect the server from abuse, it counts requests per network address for
one minute at a time. These counts are kept in memory only and are not stored
or shared.

The server runs on Microsoft Azure (Poland Central region). It does not keep
request logs.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves your last used name, your current game (so you can rejoin it if
the app closes) and your light or dark theme choice. This stays on your device,
and deleting the app removes it.

## Children

Shapka is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data
from anyone, including children.
